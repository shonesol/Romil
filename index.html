About the academy

More than drills.
A place to grow.

Romil Soccer Academy Kifamba builds players through clear coaching, consistent training and a culture of discipline and respect.

We help athletes master the basics, improve decision-making and develop the habits needed both on match day and in everyday life.

Why train with us

Four pillars of development

01

Coaching

Clear sessions that improve technique, positioning and game understanding at every level.

02

Skills

Passing, dribbling, shooting, defending and first-touch work that transfers to match play.

03

Fitness

Speed, strength, coordination and endurance so players stay sharp for the full 90 minutes.

04

Character

Discipline, respect, responsibility and sportsmanship that last long after the final whistle.
