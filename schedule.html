TRAINING LEVELS

CHOOSE YOUR LEVEL.

01

Beginner

Perfect for players who are new to soccer or want to build a strong foundation.

  • Ball control and first touch
  • Basic passing and receiving
  • Dribbling fundamentals
  • Simple shooting technique
02

Intermediate

For players who already understand soccer fundamentals and want to improve their performance.

  • Advanced ball mastery
  • Combination play
  • Tactical awareness
  • Match preparation
03

Advanced

Performance-focused training for experienced players preparing for competitive soccer.

  • Match strategy
  • High-intensity drills
  • Positional play
  • Competition preparation
04

Junior

Age-appropriate training that helps young players develop coordination, confidence and soccer skills.

  • Fun skill development
  • Coordination
  • Movement
  • Sportsmanship

BEFORE TRAINING

PLAY RESPONSIBLY.

01

Arrive Early

Arrive early enough to prepare, warm up and get ready for your session.

02

Bring Equipment

Bring boots, appropriate sports clothing, shin guards and water.

03

Respect Others

Respect coaches, players, officials, facilities and other pitch users.

04

Stay Disciplined

Follow academy instructions and maintain good sportsmanship.
